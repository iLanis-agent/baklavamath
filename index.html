Baklava math

The recipe says "one package of phyllo" and your pan is not the pan in the recipe. Baklava math checks your pan against the sheet in the box, counts the nuts and butter the area actually takes, and sizes the syrup and the diamond grid to match. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Phyllo plan - pan dimensions and layers become a sheet-fit verdict and a box count.
  • Nut fill - pan area becomes chopped-nut grams and melted-butter grams.
  • Syrup and cut - pan area becomes syrup milliliters, and the pan becomes a diamond count.

Labeled norms

Layers: about 40 phyllo sheets per pan, half under the nuts and half over. Sheets: a standard store-bought sheet is about 23 x 33 cm; a box holds about 40. Nuts: about 0.6 g of chopped nuts per cm2 of pan; butter about 0.25 g per cm2. Syrup: about 0.32 ml per cm2 of pan, split roughly 2:1:1 sugar-water-honey by volume. Labeled kitchen norms from common recipes, not rules.
